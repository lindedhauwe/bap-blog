title: How Might We questions
date: 2026-10-29T10:00
description: The questions we used to frame our ideation.
tags: [ux]
---

- How might we give everyone a success experience within 30 seconds, also non-readers?
- How might we spark curiosity about stories?
- How might we make reading a shared experience that's about family connection?
- How might we let Boektopia visitors step into a story instead of just watching it?
- How might we change the mind of people who think reading is boring and a waste of time, through an active game?
- How might we lower the threshold to start reading for children aged 6 to 12?
- How might we play with story elements without referring to one specific book?
