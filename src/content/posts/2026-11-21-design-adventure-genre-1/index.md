## What did we do?

In the adventure genre, the players sail a boat across the sea in search of a treasure. They have to work together: player 1 scoops the water out of the boat with a bucket, while player 2 steers the boat with a wheel to avoid the rocks. We designed all the assets for this scene, and, like in the other genres, the characters are references to well-known books and stories.

### The game screen

The scene starts with the boat on open water. Rocks block the way, so player 2 has to steer around them, while the waves splash water onto the boat and player 1 keeps scooping it out. Between the rocks, characters from famous stories show up: the whale from Moby Dick and the tick-tock crocodile from Peter Pan. When the players reach the end of the sea, they arrive at an island with a chest. The chest opens and a book comes out of it, which is the reward that connects the game back to reading.

## Why this direction?

A boat that is leaking while it has to avoid rocks gives both players a clear job that they understand right away, and the two jobs depend on each other: if player 2 steers badly, the boat gets hit, and if player 1 stops scooping, the boat starts sinking. This forces the players to cooperate. Sailing in search of a treasure is the classic adventure setting, and hiding well-known characters in the scene keeps it recognisable. Ending on a chest with a book in it ties the adventure back to reading.
