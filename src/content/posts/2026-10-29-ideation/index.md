## What we did

1. Everyone brainstormed alone first.
2. Then we brainstormed together and grouped the ideas.
3. We voted on the ideas we liked best.

## Top idea 1: The Magic Mirror

Inspired by the magic mirror from the fairy tale *Snow White*.

A visitor stands in front of a "magic mirror". With the buttons in front of the mirror, they change the genre of the world around them: fantasy, romance or horror. Their movements tell the story and change the environment in the mirror.

- Works for both children and adults
- Gets people moving in an interactive way

## Top idea 2: Book Runner (duo game)

A game for two players, a bit like Subway Surfers.

- **Player 1** stands in front of the screen and sees the obstacle course moving and coming closer.
- **Player 2** has to complete the course by jumping, ducking and dodging. A camera tracks their movements.
- Along the way they collect books and help each other reach the finish.
