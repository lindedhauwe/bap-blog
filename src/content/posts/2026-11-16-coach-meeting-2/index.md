## Before the meeting

We prepared some questions for Simon. The first was whether we could reserve one of the large TV screens at school for the jury presentation. The second was whether we should also copy him in on our email contact with Aluvision.

## What we told Simon

We asked about the screen and about the Aluvision correspondence. We also discussed our hours, the research we have done so far, the LED screens and whether we are on schedule.

## Feedback from Simon

**Screen for the jury.** The white screen can be rented, although it has a smaller viewing angle. The screen at the bar cannot be reserved. We will look into the screen situation further.

**Aluvision.** Simon does not need to be copied in on these emails. His role is mainly to follow our process, not to monitor external contact.

**Hours.** The 46 hours apply per person.

**Standing out.** Most groups are working in a similar space. Simon advised us to look for what makes our project unique and to excel at it, whether that is in the concept or in the execution.

**Team form from the client.** The teamwork section was rated roughly average because the client from Boektopia simply couldn't judge it. Another teacher was also looking into this and found it to be a weird question on the form, so we don't have to worry about it.

**Friday pitch.** We will be grouped per company. Simon's gut feeling is that we will have to present our concept. We are not graded on it. The purpose is to receive feedback from the others and to hear about their projects.

**Planning.** We should add a name to each task: who does what? This will make teamwork easier since everyone knows what they're responsible for.

**Research.** Our research on inclusivity and sustainability is on the right track.

**LED screens.** It may be wise to put the client's approval in writing.

**Schedule.** We will get a clearer picture on Friday, but Simon thinks we are on track.

## What we did with it

- Assign a name to every task in our planning.
- Look into the options for a screen for the jury presentation.
- Reflect on what sets our concept apart from the other groups, so we can focus on it.

## Next coaching moments

- **Friday:** pitch session, where we will present our concept and gather feedback. We will also get a better view of our progress.
