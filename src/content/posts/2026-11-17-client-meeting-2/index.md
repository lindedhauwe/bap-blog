## The meeting

The meeting was very short. We showed the client where we stand with the slides from our [meeting prep](../2026-11-16-client-meeting-prep/), and she found them useful again as a common thread. She didn't have much to say, apart from two topics.

## Feedback

### Stamp card

- The **reason** to collect a stamp isn't attractive enough yet. It's still missing *why* a family would do it.
- So the stamp card has to connect much better with the rest of the experience, or give something in return. For example, collecting your stamp could let you **take part in a contest**. But that may add a lot of extra steps.

### Aluvision

- The LED wall is mainly something for **when the client decides to use our project**.
- So we don't have to rent anything yet.

## What did we learn?

The stamp card is meant to take families from the screen to the books, but on its own a stamp isn't a reason to walk to the bookstands. We need a stronger motivation, without making the experience longer or more complicated.

## Next steps

- Find a better approach for the stamp card: a clear reason for families to go and collect their stamp.
- We still haven't heard back from Aluvision, so we'll call them, so that the price calculation and the showroom visit can happen soon.
- Our next meeting with the client is on **Tuesday 24 November at 9 a.m.**
