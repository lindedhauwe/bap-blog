- Boektopia has 80,000–100,000 visitors: readers and non-readers, families and adults.
- Boektopia has a big heart for young readers.

## Our choice

We chose **families: children (6–12) with a parent or parents**. Children rarely visit alone, so the parent is part of the experience.
