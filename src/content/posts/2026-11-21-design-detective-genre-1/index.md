so here the idea is to have a lot of book shelves to represent a library where the guilty roam around and player 2 has to catch these and then player 1 has to find clues around the room. so we started with making book shelves (bookshelves01) and then i enhanced them a bit by adding a second bookshef and adding more books and random items (bookshelves02). and i used this reference for the bookshelves (bookshelves-reference)

then i also made the clues. so these clues are based on the appearance of the guilty person, in case the player hasn't read the book yet. so the guilty person in our game is "meneer Grondijs" from "Emiel en zijn detectives". he has a long brown coat on, a bowler hat and a moustache. so the clues are a piece of brown fabric, moustache wax with a comb and footsteps on the ground (clues01). and i used these references to make the clues: footsteps-reference, fabric-reference01, fabric-reference02, moustache-wax-reference01, moustache-wax-reference02.

then i also made the controllers: a magnifying glass for player 1 and a net for player 2 (controllers01). and i used these references to make the controllers: magnifying-glass-reference, net-reference.

then to help the players remember how many objects they have to find and what they've already found, we made a small notebook where all the clues are written down (notebooks01). and then game-screen would look like this (game-screen01). hovering over the notebook will pull it up and show all the clues that have been found so far.

then for the criminals we took characters from 4 books: "meneer grondijs" from "Emiel en zijn detectives", "de grote boze walf" from "roodkapje", "Professor James Moriarty" van "Sherlock Holmes" and "kapitein haak" from "Peter Pan" (image thieves01). and i used these references: thieves-meneer-grondijs-reference,
for the wolf thieves-wolf-reference01, thieves-wolf-reference02, thieves-wolf-reference03. mind you, the first reference is a wolf made by AI in illustrator. this helped me get the right shape and proportions for the body, because i didn't find any good references online of a wolf standing on two legs.
for moriarty: thieves-moriarty-reference01, thieves-moriarty-reference02
for kapitein haak: thieves-kapitein-haak-reference01, thieves-kapitein-haak-reference02, thieves-kapitein-haak-reference03, thieves-kapitein-haak-reference04. mind you: for the last reference i again used ai in illustrator to get the pose and proportions right, because i couldn't find any good references online in that specific pose.
