## The meeting

This was our third meeting with the client. We went through the current state of the concept and she gave feedback on two things: how the books are linked to the games, and the live counter. She also promised to send us the booklist.

## Feedback

### Books as part of the experience

- Right now it feels like the books are an **afterthought** instead of an integral part of the experience.
- For example, when you dig up the objects, we could show **Asterix's head on the helmet**.
- So we should link the games much more clearly to the books, so that it's obvious that *this object comes from these books*.

### Booklist

- The client can send us a **link to their app**, where you can see which books are on which table, per zone.
- She'll also send us an **Excel file** with all the books in it.

### Live counter

- People will probably do it, and find it interesting. Visitors might like to see what others voted for, for example.
- But she isn't sure how much it will tell **her**. It's mostly a vote on which game they like best, and she doesn't see what it adds beyond that.
- We now know someone's favourite genre, but is there a more interesting question we could ask?
    - Maybe a **different question every day**, a "question of the day".
    - For example: *Where do you like to read most?*
    - That would also give them something to post on social media: "we asked this question, and these were the answers".

## What did we learn?

The books are the whole point of the project, so they can't feel like a side dish. The games need to point back to specific books in a way visitors can see right away. The live counter is only worth building if it gives the client something useful. A rotating daily question could do that, and it doubles as content for her social media.

## Next steps

- Link the objects and games more clearly to the books, for example by showing Asterix's head when the helmet is dug up.
- Use the booklist (the app link and the Excel file) once the client sends them.
- Rethink the live counter: look into a daily question instead of a genre vote.
- Our next meeting with the client is on **Tuesday 1 December in the morning**, at the same time as today.
