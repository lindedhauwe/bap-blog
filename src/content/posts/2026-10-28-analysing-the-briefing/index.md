## What we did

We read the Boektopia brief and the client presentation.

## The client's goal

Get more people (back) into reading. In the moment, the installation should above all **inspire or entertain**.

## Our choice

**Challenge 1:** an interactive installation on the event floor (Kortrijk Xpo & Antwerp Expo).

## Do's

- Robust: it must survive a serious knock
- High throughput, no long queues
- Clear link with books and reading
- Interactive: visitors get to do something
- Easy to set up, take down and set up again

## Don'ts

- No link to specific books
- No obstacles to circulation (e.g. lots of cables)
- No complicated set-ups to use

## Deliverables

Concept, working prototype tested with the target audience, pitch deck + demo, set-up and take-down instructions.

## Insight

DEVINE asks for innovative digital technology, but the client wants something robust, simple and with few cables. Our concept has to solve both.
