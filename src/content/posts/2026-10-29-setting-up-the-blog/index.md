## What did we do?

We set up a blog to document our process and progress during the bachelor thesis. We chose **Astro**, because we already built an Astro blog in the second year of DEVINE, and host it on **GitHub Pages** so our coaches can follow along at any time.

## How is it structured?

- **Blog** — separate posts with a title and date, in chronological order.
- **Filters** — every post has one or more topics: UX, Design, Development and Feedback & meetings.
- **Planning** — our phases and tasks, with their status.
- **Hours** — time tracking per day and per person.
- **Team** — who does what.
- **AI** — an overview of how we used AI, post by post.
